Meeting Actions turns messy meeting notes into a task handoff you can use. It runs on Antseed's free APIs.

Paste notes or import text. The model extracts actions, decisions and open questions, with quotes so you can check the source.

Missing owners and vague dates stay unresolved. The app does not guess who should do the work or invent a date.

You edit the fields, then choose which tasks to include. In this example, the missing owner and date are human choices.

Download a task CSV, a calendar file, or a follow-up draft. Exports are generated locally from your included tasks. Nothing is sent automatically.
